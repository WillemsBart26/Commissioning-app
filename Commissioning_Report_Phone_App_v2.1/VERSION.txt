Commissioning Report Phone App
Version: 2.1 - tested phone package
Date: 19 October 2026

Built from the working 2.0 phone rebuild and 1.3 editable-row Windows version.

Phone features:
- Installable home-screen app for Android and iPhone after HTTPS deployment.
- Offline operation after the first successful load.
- Camera or photo-library input with on-device compression.
- PDF preview, download and system sharing to Outlook, Mail or Teams.
- Editable job, notes, checklist and completion rows.
- Draft and custom layout saved only on the device.
- New report clears report data and photos but keeps the customised row template.
- In-app Android and iPhone installation instructions.
